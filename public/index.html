FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about SteamFarmer

Can I get banned for using SteamFarmer?▾

No. SteamFarmer communicates via the official Steam network protocol (steam-user) without injecting DLLs or modifying game memory, making it 100% VAC-safe.

Can I play on my PC while SteamFarmer is active?▾

Yes! The built-in Anti-Collision guard detects your desktop session immediately, pauses cloud farming so you never get disconnected, and automatically resumes when you close the game.

How does ASF's 2-hour rule work for trading cards?▾

Games under 2 hours are farmed in parallel (up to 32 at once) to reach Valve's drop threshold quickly, then farmed 1 game at a time past 2 hours for maximum card drop frequency.

Do I need to keep my computer turned on?▾

No. SteamFarmer runs 24/7 in the cloud (Render or Docker) with PostgreSQL persistence, so your hours, card drops, and Gaussian schedules continue even with your PC off.
